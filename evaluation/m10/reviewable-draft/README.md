## Assessment declared before outputs

Inspect every captured raw response, rendered draft and exact original input. Record
mechanical completion, critical semantic defects, partial findings and latency separately.
An unavailable section is honest incompleteness; it is not proof of overall success.

Critical defects include wrong facts/owners/quantities/date meaning, lost conditions,
invented or cancelled current obligations, source mixing, invented conflict claims and
missing genuine independent work. A focused review can preserve actual uncertainty or
remaining work, but a generic warning or transcript dump alone is insufficient.
Readability, redundant review and burden receive explicit partial findings when they
make the note less useful without changing its meaning. The owner must still judge
editing benefit on actual generated output; format approval does not measure that.
