# V11 scoring rubric — declared before any generated candidate output

No evaluation is authorized by this file. Agree cases, repetitions, per-arm ceilings,
total request budget and stopping rules after the final source/schema freeze.
Retain all 40 pairs; failures are outcomes, not cases to omit or silently rerun.
The original 22 are familiar. Eighteen Chief-authored additions and implementation-authored
unit responses are development/evaluation material, not independent held-out evidence.

Score each case/repetition with exact source spans: 0 wrong/missing, 1 partial, 2 correct.
Score baseline only against its transcript. Shuffle opaque labels before assessment where
practical, reporting that notes/comparison/review structure can reveal the arm. Agent
authorship and assessment do not establish independent or fully blinded measurement.

| Dimension | Full credit | Critical failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spoken commitments and agreements | Every genuine undertaking and independent agreement survives with qualifiers; unclear candidates remain visible for review | Invented executable task, lost undertaking/agreement, or unsupported commitment admission |
| Source attribution | Written facts/requests stay visibly written; spoken fields retain correct evidence and meaning | Notes become spoken tasks/decisions/prose merely through a valid T ID |
| Claim coverage and anchors | Semantic claims accounted for with exact selected spans and all original gaps/qualifiers retained | Missing meaningful text, ambiguous/overlapping spans, or a mechanically covered passage hiding multiple independent claims |
| Comparison and uncertainty | Correct consistent/conflicting/related-distinct/unmatched relation; unresolved relation visibly reviewed | Missed/invented hard conflict, uncertainty treated as agreement, or distinct periods/scopes conflated |
| Quantities and dimensions | Correct subject, amount, units, period, scope and epistemic status; absent/unresolved extraction distinguished | Incorrect attribution/conversion, invented value, or silent loss of explicit dimensions |
| Timing and owners | Supported owner or explicit uncertainty; complete relative/conditional timing | Invented owner/deadline, qualifier stripped, retraction ignored or unrelated timing borrowed |
| Usefulness and completion | Every agreed output completes; concise emphasis and actionable review | Blocked/incomplete output presented as successful, or review noise hiding commitments |

Inspect raw comparison and summary attempts, corrections and code-resolved evidence as
well as rendered output. A quote, source ID, declared commitment basis/status, valid span
map or nonempty structured field is not proof of correct meaning. Test the explicitly
authored cost-as-commitment candidate even when it has plausible new-contract metadata.
Do not count strict old-response schema incompatibility as the semantic repair.

Judge the original cases against V11 expected.json: distinct periods/scopes may use
related-distinct or truly unmatched additional, not agreement over unrelated values.
Estimate/approval and tentative/confirmed need uncertainty review when unresolved.
Support-price disputes must not dispute adjacent monitoring facts or invoice tasks.
The multi-claim case retains its invoice undertaking and full not-before timing.

Assess the added controls independently: third-person/implied/conditional commitments
remain available; proposals, factual status, examples and explicit retractions cannot
become executable checklists. Gap negation stays inside evidence; repeated literal
selection is unambiguous. A condition's negation must not erase a real undertaking.
The six review-derived additions check factual answers versus accepted tasks, accepted
request retraction, a new promise after cancellation, partially retracted coordination,
complete external timing context and approximate/not-before timing qualifiers. If partial
cancellation cannot safely isolate an action, the complete original and retraction stay
visible in non-executable review; silently losing the remaining work fails acceptance.

Acceptance requires every agreed output complete, no critical invented/lost spoken
commitment or agreement, no critical missed/invented conflict, correct source separation
and uncertainty, meaningful qualifiers/dimensions and useful output. A fail-closed
comparison may correctly preserve a prior result yet still fail this completion gate.
No new partial-publication path is assumed.

Report per-case scores, complete/blocked/unmappable outcomes, request/correction counts
by stage and arm, warnings/review, measured latency and limitations. Report unchanged
V10-response compatibility separately from authored fake responses and fresh model
quality. No tuning, selective reruns or dropping failed cases during scoring. Replay
every selected new generation from saved evidence; verify historical hashes unchanged.
Only passing quality advances current synthetic Obsidian manual acceptance; neither
offline checks nor a freeze authorize Built or merging.

Two focused-recheck additions cover mixed-owner cancellation and selected accepted
coordination with incomplete context. An explicit retraction cannot remain inside an
executable mixed-owner task; selecting a bare acceptance cannot suppress mandatory
request/retraction review that omission would have retained.
